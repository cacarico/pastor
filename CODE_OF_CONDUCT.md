# Code of Conduct

We want pastor to be a respectful, practical project for people with different
backgrounds, skill levels, employers, and tool preferences.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, direct, and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections when impact differs from intent.
- Keep criticism focused on code, docs, behavior, and tradeoffs.
- Respect boundaries around time, attention, privacy, and security-sensitive
  information.
- Credit ideas and prior work when you know the source.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or sustained personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information, secrets, logs, hostnames, or credentials.
- Dismissing security, license, provenance, or safety concerns without review.
- Repeated disruption after maintainers ask for a change in behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests;
or limit participation to protect the project and its contributors. When
possible, maintainers should explain the action taken and the path back to
constructive participation.
